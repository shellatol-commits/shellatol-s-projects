Creators can ask for the help they actually need

Each creator profile can feature a “How you can help me” list—for example, testers, translators, documentation help or collaborators. Creators choose what to request, and supporters choose whether their activity is public.

The idea

One home. Many kinds of work.

Mods, software, tools, art, experiments, writing, resources and whatever comes next.

Cursed Portals is an independent project and creator library. It is not limited to one game, medium or subject. Each creator can build a home for their work, publish updates and organize related content. The platform is free to browse and use; payments and memberships are intentionally not part of this first version.
