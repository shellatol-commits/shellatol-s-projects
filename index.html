About the platform

What is Cursed Portals?

Cursed Portals is a project discovery and creator-support platform. It is designed to host many kinds of creative work, from written projects and rich-text publications to interactive HTML, CSS and JavaScript creations.

Projects are published by approved creators and administrators. Visitors can explore the library and take part in the community through appreciation, sharing, feedback and collaboration.
