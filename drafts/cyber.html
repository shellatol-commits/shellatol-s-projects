About Cursed Portals

One home. Many kinds of work.

Cursed Portals is intended to be a community platform for independent projects and creative work, not a platform limited to one game or one type of content.

Our approach

The platform focuses on discovery, community participation and creator ownership. It is designed to give projects a public home and make it easier for people to contribute in practical, meaningful ways.

Publishing and community standards

  • Publishing is limited to authorized accounts.
  • Admins retain their administrative permissions.
  • Project owners manage their own work according to permissions.
  • Visitors can explore public projects.
  • Visitors cannot delete other people's projects.
  • Projects are not automatically published.
