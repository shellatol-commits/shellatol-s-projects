Important · Read before playing

A warning before you open the portal.

WARNING: If you do not build a frame and light the portal, your world will hard-lock and the game will crash.

Do not leave a portal unframed or unlit. Make sure you understand the portal-building process before using this mod in a world.

How to recover a locked world

If your world becomes locked or crashes because of the portal, use this troubleshooting sequence:

  1. Turn off or disable the Cursed Portals mod.
  2. Open the affected world without the mod enabled.
  3. Once the world has opened, turn off the game completely.
  4. Reopen the game with Cursed Portals enabled.

This is the troubleshooting method supplied for the mod. Back up important worlds before experimenting.

Please read the portal instructions before using the mod.

About the mod

Welcome to Cursed Portals.

Cursed Portals is a recreation of the Odd Portals mod. Its central mechanic revolves around crafting a spawnable portal, constructing the frame and lighting it.

🌀

Spawnable portals

Craft a portal item from bundled obsidian and a valid firestarter. Use the resulting item as part of your portal setup.

🔥

Light the way

Portal-related Flint and Steel recipes let you prepare the tools needed for the portal mechanics.

💎

Portal Essence

Destroy a portal to obtain Portal Essence, which can be processed into decorative and functional building pieces.

Step one · Prepare your tools

Crafting the portal tools

The crafting table is used to prepare the Flint and Steel variants and portal-related compasses.

01

Portal Flint and Steel

Use the Spawnable Nether Portal item together with the Flint and Steel in the crafting table, following the recipe's required ingredient order.

Crafting table recipe
02

Portal Compass

The portal-related compass can be crafted using the Spawnable Nether Portal item and a Compass. The Recovery Compass is also supported.

Regular Compass or Recovery Compass
Recipe order matters. These recipes have a specific ingredient order. Follow the mod's crafting recipe rather than assuming that the ingredients can be placed in any arrangement.
Step two · Build your portal

Crafting the Spawnable Portal.

The portal item is created through a sequence of obsidian bundling recipes. Follow the stages in order.

01

Bundle nine obsidian

Start by combining nine obsidian to create the first obsidian bundle.

9 × Obsidian → Bundle of 9
First bundle created
02

Upgrade the obsidian bundle

Combine the bundle of nine obsidian with five additional obsidian to create the larger bundle.

Bundle of 9 + 5 × Obsidian → Bundle of 14
Larger bundle created
03

Craft the Spawnable Nether Portal

Combine the bundle of fourteen obsidian with either a Flint and Steel or a Fire Charge.

Bundle of 14 + Flint and Steel or Fire Charge → Spawnable Portal

The Fire Charge must match the mod's accepted firestarter tag:

neoforge:firestarters

Spawnable Nether Portal obtained
Do not forget the warning. Once you are working with portals, make sure you build the required frame and light the portal. An unbuilt or unlit portal can hard-lock your world and crash the game.
Step three · Portal materials

Portal Essence

Destroying a portal gives you Portal Essence. This material can be processed in a stonecutter to create several portal-themed building pieces.

🟪

Portal Buttons

Turn Portal Essence into portal-themed buttons for your builds and mechanisms.

▥

Portal Stairs

Create stairs using the stonecutter and Portal Essence.

▰

Portal Slabs

Produce slabs for decorative builds and architectural details.

⬚

Pressure Plates

Process Portal Essence into pressure plates for your builds.

⚒

Stonecutter

The stonecutter is the workstation used to turn Portal Essence into these different forms.

✨

Reuse the material

Your portal can provide a material that continues to be useful even after the portal itself has been destroyed.
