Read Before Using Cursed Portals

Before using this mod, you must understand how its portals work and what can happen if they are not constructed correctly.

WARNING: If you do not build a portal frame and light the portal, your world will hard-lock and the game will crash.

Make sure you follow the portal construction instructions before attempting to use a portal. Do not leave an unbuilt or unlit portal in your world.

How to troubleshoot a locked world:

1. Turn off or disable the Cursed Portals mod.
2. Open the locked world without the mod enabled.
3. Close the game completely.
4. Reopen the game with Cursed Portals enabled.

This is the recommended troubleshooting method if your world becomes locked after encountering the portal issue.

Discover the Mod

Welcome to Cursed Portals.

A Minecraft mod inspired by Odd Portals, built around crafting, portal creation, and new ways to use portal materials.

What is Cursed Portals?

Cursed Portals brings a different approach to portal creation. Instead of only relying on traditional portal construction, you can craft special items that let you create spawnable portals and use Portal Essence for additional building possibilities.

What you can do

  • ✓ Craft portal-related tools.
  • ✓ Craft bundles of obsidian.
  • ✓ Create spawnable portals.
  • ✓ Obtain Portal Essence.
  • ✓ Make decorative and functional blocks.
Crafting Recipes

Portal Tools.

Craft the special portal tools using a spawnable Nether Portal item and the corresponding vanilla item.

Portal Construction

Craft your Spawnable Portal.

The portal crafting process uses bundles of obsidian. Follow the three stages in order.

01

Craft a Bundle of 9 Obsidian

Place 9 obsidian into the crafting table to create your first Bundle of 9 Obsidian.

02

Craft a Bundle of 14 Obsidian

Combine the Bundle of 9 Obsidian with 5 additional obsidian to create a Bundle of 14 Obsidian.

03

Create the Spawnable Nether Portal

Combine the Bundle of 14 Obsidian with either a Flint and Steel or a Fire Charge to craft the Spawnable Nether Portal.

Bundle of 14 Obsidian + Flint and Steel OR Fire Charge
Fire Charge ingredient tag: neoforge:firestarters
Remember: Once you use the portal, build its frame and light it. An unbuilt or unlit portal can cause a world hard-lock and game crash.
Portal Essence

Turn portals into materials.

Killing or destroying a portal gives you Portal Essence. Take that essence to a stonecutter to create four different portal-themed building pieces.
